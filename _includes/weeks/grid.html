{%- comment -%} The grid, laid out like weeks.ginatrapani.org: one continuous stream of week boxes, in rows of ~17–21 that flow across year boundaries. Rows break at 21 "box units": an empty week is 1 unit, a labelled box is .9 + (label length − 2) × .319 units (its label spans several box widths), and a labelled box that would overflow starts the next row instead. Every year opens with a birthday box (🐣 at birth, 🎂 age · year after that), public events put their label inside the week's box, and each decade starts a fresh section and row. Week index, era, place, season and events are all resolved here at build time.
