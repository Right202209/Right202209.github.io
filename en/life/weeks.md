A map of a life, one box per week. Tap a box to see where I was and what I was doing that week.

The idea is Wait But Why's [Your Life in Weeks](https://waitbutwhy.com/2014/05/life-weeks.html); the design is ported from [Gina Trapani](https://weeks.ginatrapani.org) and [Buster Benson](https://busterbenson.com/life-in-weeks).

> The boxes do not multiply because you look at them.
