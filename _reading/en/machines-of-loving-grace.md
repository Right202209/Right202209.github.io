> I think that most people are underestimating just how radical the upside of AI could be, just as I think most people are underestimating how bad the risks could be.

It takes more courage to speak of hope than of fear. Fear sounds sober; hope sounds naive.

What is rare here is that hope is treated as something to be argued for, not merely felt.
