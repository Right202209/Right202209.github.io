> The biggest lesson that can be read from 70 years of AI research is that general methods that leverage computation are ultimately the most effective, and by a large margin.

The bitterness is not in losing to the machine. It is in losing to our own cleverness.

We keep wanting to teach it everything we know, the way a parent teaches letters. It learns best precisely when we step aside.

Perhaps humility is not a virtue here. Only a fact.
